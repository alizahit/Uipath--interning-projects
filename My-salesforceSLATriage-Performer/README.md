# 🚀 Salesforce SLA Triage - Performer

This project is the **Performer** component of the Salesforce SLA Triage automation, built using the UiPath Robotic Enterprise Framework (REFramework). 

## 📖 Project Overview

**Overall Project Purpose:**  
The **Salesforce SLA Triage** automation is a single, unified RPA solution split into two components: a Dispatcher and a Performer. The overarching goal of this automation is to systematically evaluate incoming Salesforce Cases against Service Level Agreement (SLA) business rules. It automatically identifies and escalates critical or high-value customer issues (based on revenue, priority, or keywords) to the appropriate teams, ensuring rapid response times and maintaining high customer satisfaction.

**Performer Role:**  
This specific project acts as the **Performer** component of the unified solution. It consumes the queue items generated by the Dispatcher bot. It evaluates each Salesforce Case against specific business rules (revenue, priority, and keywords) to determine if it requires escalation. If escalation is needed, the bot directly updates the case in Salesforce and leaves an audit trail.

## 🛠️ Workflow Components

### 🔍 `filterCase.xaml`
Analyzes the queue item data to determine if the case should be escalated. It evaluates two main conditions:
* **High Priority & Revenue:** If the `CasePriority` is "High" AND the `AccountAnnualRevenue` is strictly greater than 5,000,000, the case is flagged for escalation with the reason `"Yüksek Ciro ve Öncelik"`.
* **Critical Keywords:** If the `CaseDescription` contains specific critical keywords (retrieved from the `Config.xlsx` file), the case is flagged for escalation with the reason `"Kritik"`.
* Outputs the final decision (`out_isEscalated`) and the reason (`out_escalationReason`).

### ☁️ `action.xaml`
Executes the required Salesforce actions for cases that require escalation, using the UiPath Integration Service:
* **Updates Record:** Changes the Case Status to `Escalated`, sets Priority to `High`, and assigns the case to a designated Escalation Owner (`EscalationID` from config).
* **Adds Feed Item:** Creates a Chatter post (FeedItem) on the Salesforce Case with the message: `"RPA Bot Tarafından Eskale Edildi. Sebep: [Escalation Reason]"`.

## ⚙️ How It Works
1. Retrieves a transaction item (Salesforce Case) from the Orchestrator Queue.
2. `filterCase.xaml` evaluates the SLA and escalation criteria based on the client's revenue and case description keywords.
3. If the case meets the escalation criteria, `action.xaml` connects to Salesforce, updates the record's ownership and status, and inserts a FeedItem to notify the relevant team.
